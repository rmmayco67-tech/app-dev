# 🎬 My Favorite Movies  

##  The Lion King  
**Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Drama  
> “Hakuna Matata!” — a phrase that means *no worries*.  
This movie tells the story of **Simba**, a young lion who must learn about responsibility, courage, and leadership. The film is filled with emotional moments, great music, and valuable life lessons.  

**Key Characters:**  
- Simba  
- Mufasa  
- Nala  
- Scar  

---

## Tarzan  
**Genre:** Animation, Action, Adventure  
Tarzan was raised by gorillas after his parents were lost in the jungle.  
He later meets **Jane**, who introduces him to the human world. The movie explores the themes of **identity, love, and belonging**.  

**Favorite Quote:**  
> “You’ll be in my heart, always.”  

**Highlights:**  
- Amazing jungle animation 
- Powerful soundtrack by *Phil Collins*   

---

##  Godzilla  
**Genre:** Action, Sci-Fi, Monster  
Godzilla is a giant creature that rises from the ocean to restore balance to nature.  
The movie is known for its **intense battles**, **massive destruction**, and **symbolism of human impact on the environment**.  

**Fun Facts:**  
- Godzilla first appeared in **1954**.  
- Often seen as both a **villain** and a **protector**.
